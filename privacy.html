We use your email address strictly to:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address to any third parties. We use secure third-party infrastructure (like Amazon Web Services) to deliver our emails, which are bound by strict data protection agreements.

4. Unsubscribe and Data Deletion

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link located at the bottom of every email. Upon unsubscribing, your email will be immediately removed from our active mailing list. To request complete deletion of your data, please contact us.
